## 3.5b player attributes
- MILESTONE: 3.5b files: `reference/{__init__,build_attributes}.py`, `reference/player_attributes.csv` (generated, 7,221 players with >= 3 matches), `reference/player_overrides.csv` (hand-kept, 350 rows: 265 with a bowling type, 350 with a batting hand), `sql/attributes.py` (`attributes_cte()`), `analysis/player_profile.py`, `tests/test_attributes.py` (32 tests).
- RULES (thresholds are constants at the top of build_attributes.py; Super overs ignored; "matches" = matches where he batted, bowled, or fielded; a player who only stood in the field and never touched the ball is invisible):
  - role: bat_share = his balls faced / his team's balls faced over his matches; bowl_share = his legal balls bowled / team's legal balls bowled. >= 4.5% and >= 6% -> all-rounder, only bowl -> bowler, else batter; < 5 matches -> unknown. (4.5% = about 5 balls per 120, 6% = about 1.2 overs per 20.) Confidence by matches (>= 30 high, >= 10 medium).
  - keeper: stumpings by the fielder / all his matches. yes = >= 3 stumpings and >= 0.06 per match (or >= 0.04 with >= 0.5 catches per match); sometimes = >= 2 and >= 0.025; else blank. A one-off stumping stays blank. Measured: no clean break in the rate (full-time 0.1-0.3, part-time 0.04-0.06), so the catch rate (keepers 0.53 per match vs other fielders 0.31, overlapping) is only a tiebreaker.
  - bat_position = median rank of first appearance (striker or non-striker) in the innings; bat_slot: <= 2 opener, <= 4 top order, 5-7 finisher if >= 35% of balls faced are in the last 5 overs else middle (<= 6), > 6 tail. Median over the whole career, so a player who moved (Narine: 8-10 early, opener later) gets a blended slot.
  - bowl_phase_role (>= 120 legal balls): powerplay >= 45%, death >= 40%, middle >= 60% of balls (biggest share wins), else all-phase.
  - bowl_kind pace/spin guess (>= 120 balls): points for spin = +3 stumped off his bowling (>= 2 and >= 1 per 1000 balls), +1/-1 caught-behind share of his wickets < 10% / >= 14% (needs 20 wickets; keeper = anyone with >= 3 stumpings), +1 middle overs >= 55%, -1 death >= 25%, -1 over-1 share >= 10%, -1 no-ball rate >= 1%. spin if >= 2. Confidence low when score is 1-2 or < 600 balls, high when >= 1200 balls.
  - bowl_type and bat_hand: NOT derivable from Cricsheet. Only in player_overrides.csv (source manual-llm: written from Claude's memory, each with a confidence). Merged view: an override bowl_type decides bowl_kind (source/confidence from the override), else the derived guess.
- MILESTONE: 3.5b accuracy of derived pace/spin (measured, `bowl_kind_confidence` shown): vs the manual seed 96.2% (255/265; thresholds were tuned on this set, so optimistic). Vs Hawk-Eye local style labels, excluding Hawk-Eye's catch-all `SEAM` label (209 bowlers, 188 with a derived guess): 91.0% overall; high confidence 100% (122), medium 100% (23), low 60% (43). Errors are all spinners guessed as pace, mostly < 600 balls. The manual seed agrees with Hawk-Eye on pace/spin for 128/128 shared bowlers where Hawk-Eye has a specific style.
